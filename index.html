Seven plants, one can. Hover to pause, or just let them turn.

01 · Fiber source

Chicory root

Roasted as a coffee stand-in back when real coffee was rationed. Here it's inulin — food for the gut bacteria you've already got.

02 · Fiber source

Cassava root

A starchy tuber that's a staple food across the tropics. In this can, it's doing most of the heavy lifting behind the 9g.

03 · Fiber source

Jerusalem artichoke

No relation to Jerusalem or artichokes — it's a sunflower's tuber, and one of the richest natural sources of inulin around.

04 · Botanical*

Nopal cactus

The paddle of the prickly pear — grilled, diced, and eaten across Mexico for centuries. Here, purely for its fiber.

05 · Botanical*

Marshmallow root

Yes — the actual plant the campfire kind was originally named after, long before it was just sugar and gelatin.

06 · Botanical*

Calendula flower

Pot marigold, in botanist terms — the same cheerful orange bloom gardeners grow for looks. Included as a botanical extract.

07 · Botanical*

Kudzu root

A vine so fast-growing it earned the nickname "the plant that ate the South." Here, only a trace botanical extract.

*Extract. "Supports Digestive Health" per the product label — not medical advice.
